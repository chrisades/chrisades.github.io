Draw2Sound

Draw2Sound is a digital synthesizer that utilizes a user-drawn waveform as its timbre/tone-quality. You can create a sound by either drawing it in the time domain, or drawing it in the frequency domain. To hear your synthesized sound, use the built-in keyboard piano or plug in any midi controller with keys. You can further shape the sound by altering the attack, release, and LPF using a midi controller. View your waveform with the built-in scope or spectrum functions..
