FaceSynth

FaceSynth was an interactive digital musical instrument built in 2024 for the “Building Sound” course I took at the Computer Music Center at Columbia University. It translates real-time facial expressions into sound by pairing computer vision with digital signal processing, turning your webcam into a touchless expression controlled synthesizer.

The system utilizes MediaPipe Face Mesh to track facial landmarks, maps those physical coordinates into control data, and streams them over a local network using Open Sound Control (OSC) to a dedicated audio engine built with the Pyo DSP library.
